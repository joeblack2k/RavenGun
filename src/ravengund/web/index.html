Rumble when shooting

The Move rumbles when you shoot. Changes apply immediately, also while you are playing.

Rumble works as soon as the lightgun is on. The Test button always works when the Move is connected.

Ball colour

The camera follows the glowing ball on the Move. Choose a colour that does not occur in the room.

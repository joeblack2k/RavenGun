Calibration

This screen guides you through the measurements. Everything is done with the gun.

  1. Magnetometer turn slowly in circles for about a minute
  2. Steady aim hold still on the cross seven times for five seconds
  3. Buttons test and record which button is which
  4. Screen the nine targets (the calculation comes later)
  5. Practice aim and shoot targets to feel how it aims

The buttons you can use now are shown at the bottom of the screen.

Magnetometer

  1. Hold it as when playing, with the Move in the Sharp Shooter
  2. Where to stand at least 1 metre from metal, speakers and the TV
  3. Turn slowly in all directions: figure eights, up and down, roll around the barrel, upside down too
  4. How long at least 40 seconds, until the ball is green. Do not shake.

    Steady aim

    1. Cross a cross is shown in the middle of the TV; hold the gun in a playing position
    2. Five times aim at the cross, pull the trigger and hold still for five seconds
    3. Left and right then half a metre to the left, and to the right, and the same again
    4. Recording the daemon records everything with labels. Too restless? Then that measurement is repeated.

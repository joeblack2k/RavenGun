The scripts that produced dropin/eerste-plt-slots.tsv (2026-10-13). Research material, not delivery.
See ../EERSTE-PLT-SLOTS.md for the method and its limits.

  slots.py, elfp.py   parse the dumped image through PT_DYNAMIC (there are no section headers) and list the first-PLT stubs
  b7.py, hist.py      every 0xb7... dword in the image, classified by where it sits
  align.py            fit a candidate library against the stale addresses (page-aligned base, runs of consecutive matches)
  interp.py, interp2.py  the piecewise fit for libc (the cabinet's build is near 2.19-0ubuntu6, not identical)
  callers.py          direct call references per stub (used as a second opinion on a name)
  glnames.py          the libGL names against the NVIDIA driver's export table
  mktable.py          writes the table: mktable.py <workdir> <game> <dir with the unpacked packages>

The packages the names were read from are NOT in this repository (181 MB, public downloads). They were only read with

  libtheora0 1.1.1+dfsg.1-3.2, libglu1-mesa 9.0.0-2 and 9.0.0-2.1, freeglut3 2.8.1-1 and 2.8.1-2, nvidia-331 331.38-0ubuntu7
